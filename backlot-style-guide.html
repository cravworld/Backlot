03 · Signature

The strip

Traditional stripboard scheduling color-codes each scene on a paper strip so the whole day is scannable at a glance. Every card and row in Backlot carries that same colored spine — and the color always means the same thing, wherever it appears: call sheets, permits, rights obligations, dispatch queues.
